FROM CRISIS TO ACTION

We turn scattered requests into clear priorities.

In a disaster, dozens of shelters can ask for help at the same time. Our system turns those requests into an actionable dispatch queue.

01

People need help

A shelter reports location, victims, waiting time and supplies needed.

02

Requests are prioritized

Urgency is calculated so the most critical requests rise to the top.

03

Requests are verified

Relief staff review the reported situation and verify the request before assistance is coordinated.

04

Relief is coordinated

Verified requests are matched with suitable relief organizations that can respond.
