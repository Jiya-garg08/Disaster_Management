Terms & Conditions
  1. All information, credentials and documents submitted in this form must be accurate, current and belong to the organization registering. Submitting false or misleading details may result in permanent rejection from the Relief Partner Network.
  2. Registration alone does not verify or approve your organization. Relief Resolver staff will review your DARPAN ID, PAN and uploaded documents before your organization can receive or respond to any emergency request.
  3. Your organization is responsible for keeping its contact details, service offerings and availability status up to date so that requests are matched correctly.
  4. Relief Resolver acts only as a coordination platform connecting requests with relief organizations. It is not responsible for the quality, timeliness or outcome of assistance provided by any partner organization.
  5. Uploaded documents and organization data will be used solely for verification and coordination purposes and will not be shared outside the review process without consent.
  6. Relief Resolver reserves the right to suspend or remove any organization from the network at its discretion, including for inaccurate information, inactivity, or reports of misconduct.

Why verification matters

Relief Resolver does not automatically connect emergency requests to unverified organizations. Every organization must first be reviewed by the Relief Coordination Centre.
